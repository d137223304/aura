  <div class="text-center mb-12">
    <h1 class="text-5xl font-serif font-bold text-brand-dark !mb-4">Shipping Information</h1>
    <p class="text-xl text-gray-600 font-serif">Delivering Your Treasures, Worldwide.</p>
  </div>

  <p class="text-lg mb-8">At Vintique, we are committed to delivering your newly acquired luxury items safely and efficiently, wherever you are in the world. We are based in the USA and partner with reliable carriers to ensure your order reaches you in excellent condition.</p>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Order Processing</h2>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mb-8">
    <li><strong>Processing Time:</strong> Orders are typically processed and prepared for shipment within 1-3 business days after payment confirmation. During peak seasons or sales events, processing times may be slightly longer.</li>
    <li><strong>Order Verification:</strong> For security purposes, some orders may be subject to additional verification which can cause delays. We appreciate your understanding.</li>
    <li><strong>Business Days:</strong> Our business days are Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Domestic Shipping (USA)</h2>
  <div class="space-y-4 mb-8">
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-brand-dark mb-2">Standard Shipping</h3>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Carrier:</strong> Typically USPS Priority Mail, FedEx Ground, or UPS Ground.</li>
        <li><strong>Estimated Delivery:</strong> 3-7 business days after processing.</li>
        <li><strong>Cost:</strong> Calculated at checkout based on order value and destination. We may offer free standard shipping on orders over a certain value (e.g., $500).</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-brand-dark mb-2">Expedited Shipping</h3>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Carrier:</strong> Typically USPS Priority Mail Express, FedEx 2Day, or UPS 2nd Day Air.</li>
        <li><strong>Estimated Delivery:</strong> 1-3 business days after processing.</li>
        <li><strong>Cost:</strong> Calculated at checkout.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">International Shipping</h2>
  <p class="mb-4">We are pleased to offer shipping to most countries worldwide.</p>
  <div class="space-y-4 mb-8">
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-brand-dark mb-2">Standard International Shipping</h3>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Carrier:</strong> Typically DHL Express, FedEx International, or UPS Worldwide Saver. We choose the most reliable carrier for your destination.</li>
        <li><strong>Estimated Delivery:</strong> Varies by destination, typically 5-15 business days after processing. Please note that customs clearance can cause additional delays.</li>
        <li><strong>Cost:</strong> Calculated at checkout based on destination, order value, and package weight/dimensions.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-brand-dark mb-2">Duties, Taxes, and Customs Fees</h3>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
        <li>International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and/or customs processing fees, which are levied once a shipment reaches your country.</li>
        <li><strong>The recipient of the shipment is the importer of record and is responsible for all such import fees, as well as complying with all laws and regulations of the destination country.</strong> Vintique does not collect these fees at checkout.</li>
        <li>We recommend contacting your local customs office for information on potential charges before placing your order.</li>
        <li>If an international shipment is refused by the recipient due to unpaid duties/taxes or other reasons, any return shipping costs and original shipping charges will be deducted from the refund.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">All Shipments</h2>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mb-8">
    <li><strong>Tracking:</strong> Once your order ships, you will receive a shipment confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your package on the carrier's website. You can also use our [Track Your Order](/track-your-order/) page.</li>
    <li><strong>Insurance:</strong> All shipments are insured for their full value to protect against loss or damage during transit.</li>
    <li><strong>Signature Required:</strong> For security and to ensure safe delivery, most orders (especially high-value items) will require a direct signature upon delivery.</li>
    <li><strong>Address Accuracy:</strong> Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer. If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete address, additional shipping fees may apply to reship the order.</li>
    <li><strong>P.O. Boxes:</strong> We may not be able to ship to P.O. Box addresses for high-value items or via certain carriers. Please provide a physical street address. For APO/FPO addresses, please contact us prior to ordering.</li>
    <li><strong>Lost or Damaged Packages:</strong> In the rare event that your package is lost or damaged in transit, please contact us immediately at [Your Customer Service Email] so we can initiate a claim with the carrier. Please retain all packaging materials if your item arrives damaged.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Questions?</h2>
  <p>If you have any questions about our shipping policy or need assistance with your order, please don't hesitate to <a href="/contact-us/" class="text-brand-gold hover:underline">contact us</a> or email us at [Your Customer Service Email].</p>
