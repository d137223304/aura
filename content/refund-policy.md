  <p class="text-lg mb-8">At Vintique, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. As we specialize in pre-owned luxury items, we strive to provide detailed descriptions, accurate photographs, and a thorough [Authenticity Guarantee](/guarantee/). Please review the following policy regarding returns and refunds.</p>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">General Return Eligibility</h2>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mb-8">
    <li><strong>Return Window:</strong> You may request a return within [e.g., 7 or 14] days of the delivery date.</li>
    <li><strong>Item Condition:</strong> To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with all original tags, dust bags, authenticity cards, and accessories intact and included. The security tag, if attached, must also be intact and untampered with.</li>
    <li><strong>Non-Returnable Items:</strong>
        <ul>
            <li>Items marked as "Final Sale."</li>
            <li>Items that have been used, altered, or damaged after receipt.</li>
            <li>Items returned without all original packaging and accompaniments.</li>
            <li>Returns initiated outside the specified return window.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li><strong>Authenticity Concerns:</strong> If you have a concern about the authenticity of an item you received, please contact us immediately. We take such concerns very seriously and will work with you to resolve them in accordance with our Authenticity Guarantee.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">How to Initiate a Return</h2>
  <ol class="list-decimal pl-5 space-y-3 mb-8">
    <li>
      <strong>Contact Us:</strong> To start a return, please contact our Customer Service team at [Your Customer Service Email] or via our <a href="/contact-us/" class="text-brand-gold hover:underline">Contact Us</a> page within the return window. Please provide your order number and the reason for your return.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Return Authorization:</strong> If your return is eligible, we will provide you with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and detailed instructions on how to send your item back to us. Please do not send items back without an RMA number, as they may not be accepted.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Packaging Your Return:</strong> Securely package the item in its original packaging (or equivalent protective packaging) to prevent damage during transit. Include all original accompaniments.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Shipping Your Return:</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>You will be responsible for paying for your own shipping costs for returning your item, unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong item sent, item not as described in a way that materially affects its value or use beyond normal pre-owned condition).</li>
        <li>We strongly recommend using a trackable and insured shipping method, as we are not responsible for returns lost or damaged in transit.</li>
        <li>Clearly mark the RMA number on the outside of the package.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ol>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Inspection and Refunds</h2>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mb-8">
    <li><strong>Inspection:</strong> Once we receive your returned item, our team will inspect it to ensure it meets the return eligibility criteria. This process may take [e.g., 3-5] business days.</li>
    <li><strong>Notification:</strong> We will notify you via email once your return has been received and inspected, and whether your refund has been approved.</li>
    <li><strong>Approved Refunds:</strong> If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within [e.g., 7-10] business days. Please note that it may take some time for your bank or credit card company to process and post the refund.</li>
    <li><strong>Deductions:</strong> Original shipping costs are non-refundable. If you received free shipping on your order, the actual outbound shipping cost we incurred may be deducted from your refund. For international returns where duties/taxes were refused by the recipient, these costs will also be deducted.</li>
    <li><strong>Denied Returns:</strong> If a returned item does not meet our return eligibility criteria (e.g., signs of use, missing parts, damaged security tag), we reserve the right to deny the refund or issue a partial refund. In such cases, we will contact you to discuss options, which may include returning the item to you at your expense.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Exchanges</h2>
  <p class="mb-8">Due to the unique nature of pre-owned items, we typically do not offer direct exchanges. If you wish to exchange an item, we recommend returning the original item for a refund (if eligible) and placing a new order for the desired item.</p>

  <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-10 !mb-6">Questions?</h2>
  <p>If you have any questions about our Refund & Return Policy, please <a href="/contact-us/" class="text-brand-gold hover:underline">contact us</a> or email us at [Your Customer Service Email] before making a purchase.</p>
