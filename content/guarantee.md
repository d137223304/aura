  <div class="bg-brand-gray p-8 rounded-lg my-10 shadow">
    <h2 class="text-3xl font-serif font-semibold text-brand-dark !mt-0 !mb-6 text-center">Our Rigorous Authentication Process</h2>
    <p class="mb-6 text-center">Before any item is listed on our website, it undergoes a meticulous and comprehensive multi-point inspection by our team of highly trained, in-house authentication experts. Our specialists have years of experience and are deeply familiar with the unique characteristics and craftsmanship details of each luxury brand we carry.</p>

    <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-brand-dark mb-3">Key Aspects of Our Verification:</h3>
    <ul class="list-disc pl-6 space-y-3 mb-6">
      <li><strong>Materials & Craftsmanship:</strong> We scrutinize the quality of leather, canvas, exotic skins, stitching, seams, and overall construction, comparing them against brand-specific standards.</li>
      <li><strong>Hardware:</strong> Engravings, zippers, clasps, studs, and other hardware are examined for correct markings, weight, finish, and operational smoothness.</li>
      <li><strong>Stamping & Branding:</strong> We inspect date codes, serial numbers (where applicable), brand logos, and "Made In" stamps for correct font, placement, depth, and consistency with authentic examples.</li>
      <li><strong>Lining & Interior:</strong> Interior materials, stitching, pockets, and brand tags are carefully checked.</li>
      <li><strong>Symmetry & Proportions:</strong> The overall shape, dimensions, and symmetry of the bag are assessed.</li>
      <li><strong>Accompanying Items:</strong> Dust bags, authenticity cards, booklets, and boxes (if included) are also reviewed for consistency with the brand's standards, though their presence or absence does not solely determine authenticity of the bag itself.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We utilize a combination of hands-on expertise and, where appropriate, advanced authentication technology and third-party expert consultation to ensure the highest level of accuracy.</p>
  </div>

    <li><strong>Financial Protection:</strong> In the extremely unlikely event that an item purchased from Vintique is determined to be non-authentic by a verified, reputable third-party authenticator (subject to our review and conditions outlined below), we will offer you a full refund, including all shipping costs.</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="mb-8">Please note that opinions from individuals not recognized as professional authenticators, or from retail store associates (who are generally trained in sales, not formal authentication of pre-owned goods), may not be considered sufficient proof for an authenticity claim.</p>
